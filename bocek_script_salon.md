**Hüseyin** — sıska, Fenerbahçe formalı, altın zincirli. **Yiğit** —
Galatasaray formalı, titiz.

Espri Yiğit'in tepkisizliğinde. İkisi sırayla küfrederken o hiç bozulmadan
oturmaya devam ediyor — utanırsa veya gülerse şaka ölüyor.

# TEK BÖLÜM

> Interior of a modest Turkish living room at night, built at cockroach scale:
> a worn fabric sofa with lace armrest covers, a patterned carpet, a glowing
> television, a coffee table with tea glasses, warm lamp light.
>
> Single continuous wide shot holding the doorway and the sofa together.
> YİĞİT, a neat careful cockroach in a yellow and red halved football jersey,
> sits back on the sofa beside HÜSEYİN, a wiry skinny cockroach in a navy blue
> and bright yellow vertically striped jersey with a thin gold chain. SAIT, a
> large older cockroach in the same navy and yellow striped jersey with a
> chipped left antenna, walks in through the doorway carrying a tray of tea.
>
> The moment Sait steps into the room, Yiğit shifts his weight on the sofa and
> lets out a long loud brassy fart. The tea glasses rattle faintly.
>
> Sait stops dead in the doorway, rears back, screws his face up and shouts:
> "Oğlum sen ne yedin amına koyayım!"
>
> Hüseyin yanks the collar of his jersey up over his mouth, waves a front leg
> frantically in front of his face and shouts over him: "Siktir lan Yiğit, evi
> batırdın!"
>
> Through all of it Yiğit does not move a muscle. He stays settled on the
> sofa, calm and entirely unbothered, and reaches for his tea. Hold on the
> three of them as the shot ends.
>
> SFX: one long loud fart, glasses rattling, two overlapping shouts. Ambient:
> quiet television murmur.
>
> Ultra-realistic cinematic 3D animation, photoreal chitin texture, Pixar-level
> facial expression and lip sync, expressive eyes, shallow depth of field,
> warm practical lighting, film grain, stable facial features, 16:9 widescreen.

## Üretirken

- **Osuruğu ses olarak tarif etmek şart.** `SFX` satırını silmeyin, yoksa
  model sessiz üretiyor ve sahne anlamsız kalıyor.
- **Görünür duman/bulut yazmadım** bilerek — fotogerçekçi üslupta çizgi film
  gibi duruyor. Tepkiler kokuyu zaten anlatıyor.
- **Yiğit hiç bozulmamalı.** Prompttaki `does not move a muscle` ve
  `entirely unbothered` ifadeleri esprinin tamamı.
- **Küfür reddedilirse:** Sait `Oğlum sen ne yedin ya!`, Hüseyin
  `Kes şunu lan Yiğit, evi batırdın!`
- **Üçü tek kadroda** — kapı ve kanepe aynı planda olmalı ki Sait'in girişi
  ile Yiğit'in hareketi aynı anda görünsün.
