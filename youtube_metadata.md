## Description

Before Islamic history entered one of its most painful chapters, there was a man whose greatness was shaped by courage, knowledge, faith, and sacrifice — Ali ibn Abi Talib, the fourth caliph of Islam, cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet Muhammad, and one of the most important figures in Islamic history.

In this episode of Story of Geniuses, we follow Ali's life from his childhood close to the Prophet, through the danger of the Hijrah, his courage in battle, his years of knowledge and wisdom, and the painful trials of his caliphate — a story of faith, leadership, and sacrifice.

This is a Level 3 English documentary: clear language, steady rhythm, and meaningful ideas to help you learn history while improving your English.
