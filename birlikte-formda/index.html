One free meal a week: eat out together on whichever day suits you. Sait: 1.5× protein and carbs. Serena: plate method, half portion of bread or rice （餐盘法，面包或米饭半份）.

Fixed breakfast: Nomadic Clusters (169 g)

Yoghurt + oat clusters, quick and practical. The only thing it's short on is protein: about 8–10 g on its own (check the pack).

  • Sait: add 2 boiled eggs or a glass of milk or kefir. That takes breakfast to 25+ g protein.
  • Serena: enough on its own; if you're hungry, add 1 boiled egg. 单吃就够了；如果饿，加一个水煮蛋。

Lunch box tips

  • Cool cooked rice quickly (spread it out) and get the boxes in the fridge within an hour. Reheat until steaming hot all the way through.
  • Batch boxes keep 3 days in the fridge, so batch 1 covers Monday–Tuesday and batch 2 the next two work days.
  • Fish in a shared office microwave isn't popular with colleagues; keep salmon and sea bass for dinners.
  • Keep sauces, dips and salad leaves in a small separate pot and add them after heating.
  • Label the boxes S and Se (or two colours) so the bigger ones go to Sait.

First day at the gym

  • If there's staff on, they can show you how to set up the machines in 5 minutes. Just ask.
  • Set the seat so the handles are at chest height and your knees line up with the machine's pivot point.
  • Tempo: lift in 1 second, lower with control in 2. Don't hold your breath; breathe out as you lift.
  • On the leg press, don't lock your knees at the top; keep a slight bend.
  • Mild muscle soreness in the first week is normal; it fades in 2–3 days and gets less each week.
  • Comfortable, supportive trainers and a water bottle are all you need.
