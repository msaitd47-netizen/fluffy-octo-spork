Ready-meal day (Wednesday or Friday)

Check the front of the pack. A good pick for:

  • Sait: 500–650 kcal and at least 30 g protein.
  • Serena: 350–500 kcal and at least 20 g protein. 选择 350–500 千卡、蛋白质至少 20 克的即食餐。
  • Good choices: chicken, beef or fish with rice or noodles and veg; chilli con carne; chicken tikka with rice; teriyaki or Thai chicken rice bowls; the "high protein" ranges at Tesco and Dunnes.
  • Less often: creamy pasta bakes, pies, lasagne and katsu curry. They're filling but heavier and lower in protein.
  • Still hungry? Add a piece of fruit, a yoghurt pot or a bag of salad.

First day at the gym

  • If there's staff on, they can show you how to set up the machines in 5 minutes. Just ask.
  • Set the seat so the handles are at chest height and your knees line up with the machine's pivot point.
  • Tempo: lift in 1 second, lower with control in 2. Don't hold your breath; breathe out as you lift.
  • On the leg press, don't lock your knees at the top; keep a slight bend.
  • Mild muscle soreness in the first week is normal; it fades in 2–3 days and gets less each week.
  • Comfortable, supportive trainers and a water bottle are all you need.
