9月19日 – 26日 · 都柏林出发 · 7晚 · 全程不自驾

爱琴海到卡帕多西亚

From the Aegean to Cappadocia · 19–26 September, 7 nights, no driving

土耳其九月行程。三个非去不可的地方——卡帕多西亚、棉花堡、以弗所——是这趟旅程的骨架；安塔利亚是家人的城市。除了热气球那天，每天早上都可以睡到自然醒。

Turkey in September. Three must-sees — Cappadocia, Pamukkale and Ephesus — form the backbone; Antalya is the family stop. Apart from the balloon morning, you can sleep in every single day.

路线

The route

塞尔丘克 Selçuk ×2→棉花堡 Pamukkale ×1→安塔利亚 Antalya ×1→卡帕多西亚 Cappadocia ×2→伊兹密尔 İzmir ×1

一条单向的线：从西边的爱琴海往南到地中海，再往东进内陆，最后飞回伊兹密尔。不走回头路。中间还有两班土耳其国内航班：9月23日安塔利亚→开塞利，9月25日开塞利→伊兹密尔，都是直飞一个半小时。

One line that never doubles back: west from the Aegean, south to the Mediterranean, east into the interior, then a hop back to İzmir for the flight home. Two domestic flights in the middle — Antalya→Kayseri on the 23rd, Kayseri→İzmir on the 25th, both direct, ~1.5 hours.

每日行程

Day by day

19周六 Sat

都柏林 → 塞尔丘克（深夜抵达）

Dublin → Selçuk, arriving late

白天在都柏林可以慢慢收拾，傍晚6点40分的飞机。凌晨1点05分落地伊兹密尔，有人来机场接我们，开一小时到塞尔丘克——一个安静的小镇，我们住两晚。到酒店大概两点半，直接睡。

A free day in Dublin to pack slowly; the flight leaves at 18:40. We land in İzmir at 01:05, where a friend's driver meets us for the hour's drive to Selçuk — a quiet little town where we stay two nights. Into the hotel around 02:30, straight to bed.

20周日 Sun

希林杰 + 以弗所 🏛️

Şirince village + Ephesus

昨晚睡得晚，今天睡到自然醒。中午去希林杰（8公里，有小巴）——山上的石头房子村庄，以水果酒出名，在这里吃一顿长长的午饭。

We got in late, so sleep as long as you like. Around midday we head to Şirince (8 km, local minibus) — a hillside village of stone houses famous for its fruit wines, and a long lunch there.

下午3点去以弗所（离酒店只有3公里）。这是地中海保存最完好的古城：塞尔苏斯图书馆的立面、能坐两万五千人的古剧场、脚下的大理石街道。邮轮团下午就回船了，人少；而且下午的金色阳光打在图书馆上，比早上还好看。附近还有阿尔忒弥斯神庙遗址（古代世界七大奇迹之一）。傍晚回镇上吃饭。

At 15:00 we go to Ephesus, only 3 km away — the best-preserved ancient city in the Mediterranean: the façade of the Library of Celsus, a theatre that seated 25,000, marble streets underfoot. The cruise groups head back to their ships in the afternoon, so it empties out — and the golden late light on the Library is better than morning. Nearby are the ruins of the Temple of Artemis, one of the Seven Wonders. Dinner back in town.

21周一 Mon

塞尔丘克 → 棉花堡 ⚪

Selçuk → Pamukkale

上午不赶时间。吃完早餐坐长途大巴去代尼兹利（约3小时20分），再转20分钟小巴到棉花堡村，下午5点左右到。

No rush in the morning. After breakfast, a coach to Denizli (~3h20), then a 20-minute minibus to Pamukkale village, arriving around 17:00.

🏊 克利奥帕特拉古池——这个一定要下水。全年恒温36°C，池底躺着公元7世纪大地震震塌的古罗马石柱，你就在这些两千年的大理石中间游泳。有更衣室和储物柜。

🏊 Cleopatra's Antique Pool — don't skip this one. A steady 36°C all year, with Roman columns lying on the bottom where a 7th-century earthquake dropped them. You swim among two-thousand-year-old marble. Changing rooms and lockers on site.

游完泳再往上走去棉花堡的白色梯田。必须赤脚进入（规定），温泉水是暖的。日落前的最后一个半小时，雪白的钙化梯田会变成橙金色——旅行团这个点早就走光了，这是住在村里才有的待遇。晚上在村里吃饭，酒店有温泉泳池。

Then we walk up the white terraces of Pamukkale itself. You must go barefoot (it's the rule), and the water is warm. In the last 90 minutes before closing the white travertines turn orange and gold — the tour buses are long gone by then. This is the reward for staying in the village. Dinner locally, and the hotel has a thermal pool.

22周二 Tue

泡温泉的早上 → 安塔利亚看海 🌊

A morning in the thermal pool → the sea at Antalya

上午整个都留给温泉泳池。9点到10点之间坐大巴去安塔利亚（约3.5小时，翻过托罗斯山脉），中午1点左右到。住在卡莱伊奇老城——奥斯曼时期的木房子、窄石板路、游艇码头，整个老城建在临海的悬崖上。

The whole morning is for the thermal pool. Between 09:00 and 10:00 we take the coach to Antalya (~3.5h over the Taurus mountains), arriving around 13:00. We stay in Kaleiçi, the old town — Ottoman wooden houses, narrow lanes, a yacht harbour, the whole quarter sitting on cliffs above the sea.

下午3点到5点：坐船。从老城码头出发，沿着悬崖走，能看到下杜登瀑布直接从崖上落进海里——土耳其只有这一处。

15:00–17:00: a boat trip from the old harbour, along the cliffs to the point where the Lower Düden waterfall drops straight into the sea — the only place in Turkey where that happens.

🏊 傍晚去孔亚阿尔特海滩，坐复古有轨电车过去。背后是贝伊山脉，面前是地中海，太阳正好落在海面上。九月底海水26–27°C——整个夏天晒暖的水，比都柏林的空气还热，海滩有淋浴和更衣室，带泳衣。晚上和他的家人一起吃饭。

🏊 Late afternoon at Konyaaltı beach, reached by the vintage tram. The Beydağları mountains behind you, the open Mediterranean in front, and the sun sets directly over the water. The sea is 26–27°C in late September — warmer than the air in Dublin — and there are showers and changing cabins, so bring a swimsuit. Dinner with his family in the evening.

23周三 Wed

安塔利亚 → 卡帕多西亚

Antalya → Cappadocia

上午在老城走走：哈德良门、宣礼塔、从城墙看地中海。想再下一次水的话，老城里就有一个小海滩，四十五分钟就够。中午的飞机飞开塞利，再一小时车程到格雷梅。我们住洞穴酒店——房间是直接在岩石里凿出来的。

A morning walk through the old town: Hadrian's Gate, the fluted minaret, the Mediterranean seen from the walls. If you want one more swim, there's a small beach inside the old town itself — 45 minutes is enough. A midday flight to Kayseri, then an hour by road to Göreme, where we stay in a cave hotel — rooms carved directly into the rock.

傍晚去乌奇希萨尔城堡俯瞰整片地区，日落去红谷——岩石会真的变成红色。晚饭吃陶罐烤肉：肉在密封的陶罐里烤熟，上桌时服务员当着你的面用小锤把罐子敲开。早点睡，明天要早起。

Late afternoon at Uçhisar Castle for the view over the whole region, then sunset in the Red Valley, where the rock really does turn red. Dinner is testi kebabı — meat slow-cooked in a sealed clay pot that the waiter cracks open at your table with a little hammer. Early night: tomorrow starts early.

24周四 Thu

热气球的早晨 🎈

Balloon morning

凌晨4点45分起床——全程唯一一次早起。热气球公司会来酒店接，飞行约一小时，落地后有香槟，8点就回来吃早饭了。

Up at 04:45 — the only early start of the whole trip. The balloon company collects us from the hotel, the flight is about an hour, there's champagne on landing, and we're back for breakfast by 08:00.

关于害怕这件事 · IF YOU'RE NERVOUS 🎈

很多人第一次都紧张，但热气球和想象中不一样：吊篮很大，边沿到肩膀高，站在里面是「被围住」的感觉，不是悬空。最神奇的是——因为你跟着风一起走，所以完全感觉不到风。没有发动机、没有震动、没有突然的移动，只有偶尔的喷火声和一片安静。很多恐高的人说比坐玻璃电梯还稳。唯一有点颠的是落地，飞行员会提前提醒。

Almost everyone is nervous the first time, but a balloon is nothing like what people imagine. The basket is large and its sides come up to your shoulders — it feels enclosed, not exposed. The strangest part is that you feel no wind at all, because you travel with it. No engine, no vibration, no sudden movement — just the occasional roar of the burner and then complete silence. People who are afraid of heights often find it steadier than a glass lift. The only bumpy moment is the landing, and the pilot warns you first.

而且随时可以改主意，前一天晚上、甚至当天凌晨都可以。不飞也完全没关系：酒店的露台就是最好的观景位——那些著名的照片，大多就是从露台或者红谷拍的，几百个气球同时升起来。一个在天上拍，一个在地上拍，两边都好。

And you can change your mind at any point — the night before, or even at 04:45 on the morning itself. Not flying is genuinely fine: the hotel terrace is the best seat in the house. Most of those famous photographs are taken from a terrace or from the Red Valley, with hundreds of balloons rising at once. One of us shoots from above, one from below — both of us win.

吃完早餐回房间补觉。这一天下午故意留空了，可以泡酒店泳池，也可以慢慢逛：格雷梅露天博物馆（走路15分钟，凿在岩石里的拜占庭教堂，满墙壁画）和阿瓦诺斯的陶艺工作坊（小巴20分钟，可以自己上手拉坯——这里做陶的传统有几千年了，和中国的瓷器算是遥远的亲戚）。晚上在葡萄酒露台看日落。

After breakfast, go back to bed. The afternoon is deliberately left empty: the hotel pool, or a slow wander to the Göreme Open-Air Museum (15 minutes on foot — Byzantine churches cut into the rock, walls covered in frescoes) and the pottery workshops of Avanos (20 minutes by minibus, where you can throw a pot yourself; the tradition here is thousands of years old, a distant cousin of Chinese porcelain). Sunset from a wine terrace in the evening.

25周五 Fri

飞伊兹密尔，海边的傍晚

Fly to İzmir, an evening by the water

如果昨天因为风大取消了热气球，今天早上是备用场次。中午飞伊兹密尔，下午到，住在海边长廊旁的阿尔桑贾克。傍晚沿着科尔登海滨长廊散步——太阳落进伊兹密尔湾，路上有马车。晚上吃meze（土耳其式的一桌小菜，配茴香酒），这是这趟旅程的告别晚餐。

If yesterday's flight was cancelled for wind, this morning is the backup slot. A midday flight to İzmir, arriving in the afternoon; we stay in Alsancak, right by the waterfront. In the late afternoon we walk the Kordon promenade — the sun drops into the bay and horse-drawn carriages pass by. Dinner is meze, a table full of small dishes with rakı — our farewell meal.

26周六 Sat

伊兹密尔 → 都柏林

İzmir → Dublin

上午在科尔登吃一顿伊兹密尔式早餐：boyoz（当地的酥皮点心）配水煮蛋和红茶。然后去科纳克广场看钟楼，在凯梅拉尔蒂大巴扎买最后的手信。中午12点半去机场（轻轨25分钟）→ 下午3点05分起飞，5点50分回到都柏林。

An İzmir breakfast on the Kordon: boyoz, the local flaky pastry, with a boiled egg and black tea. Then Konak Square and its clock tower, and last souvenirs in the Kemeraltı bazaar. To the airport at 12:30 (25 minutes on the light rail) → 15:05 departure, home in Dublin by 17:50.

💳 关于支付 · ABOUT PAYING

土耳其不能用微信支付和支付宝。请提前准备好可以在境外刷的信用卡（Visa/Mastercard），再换一点土耳其里拉现金——小巴、集市、给小费的时候用得上。其他地方刷卡都没问题。

WeChat Pay and Alipay do not work in Turkey. Bring a Visa or Mastercard that works abroad, plus some Turkish lira in cash for minibuses, markets and tips. Cards are accepted almost everywhere else.

Practical notes
